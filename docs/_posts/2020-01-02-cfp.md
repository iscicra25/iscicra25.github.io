title: "Call for Posters"
---

We invite submissions from a range of topics related to the design of safe control from imprecise safety specifications, including but not limited to, from natural language, from demonstrations, in terms of sensor anomalies, or through semantic concepts. Topics of interest include the formulation of mathematical representations of such safety specifications, the quantification of the ambiguity in safe set specifications, the human-machine interaction on specifying safety concerns, and the autonomous interpretation of safety consideration from abstractly specified safety tasks. Priority will be given to posters that bridge the gap between “intangible” safety specifications to actionable safe control.

Poster proposals should be 2-4 pages in length, outlining the results being presented, their novelty, and their relevance to the workshop. All papers must be submitted in PDF and must follow the ICRA double column format. Information and templates are available [here](https://ras.papercept.net/conferences/support/support.php). The review committee will judge the contributions based on the following questions:

1. What is the approach for interpreting imprecise safety specifications and how can the results lead to actionable control formulation?
2. How is the ambiguity quantified?
3. How novel is the proposed approach and how likely it will eventually lead to a publication with impact?
4. What is the key research question that the community should address in future work and whose resolution will significantly impact your work?

### Recent PhD talk


Targeting PhD candidates who are close to graduation + Postdoc researchers. We seek a two-page research statement containing a high-level description of the research problem(s) that the applicant is addressing in their current and future studies. The research statement must have an explicit title, but there is no requirement to include an abstract section given the page limit. The research statement is expected to contain and will be evaluated for the following: 1) Motivation behind the research question(s) addressed in the applicant's studies. 2) Clarity in defining and scoping the problem. 3) The connection with the theme of the workshop. 4) A brief review of related work on the aforementioned research question(s). 5) A description of approaches contributed by the applicant, their novelty and potential advantages over existing ones. 6) An overview of the future research directions. Good examples of these research statements can be found at the [RSS Pioneer Workshop](https://sites.google.com/view/rsspioneers2025/apply?authuser=0).
